Who provides APM?

APM – My Garage is an independently developed vehicle-record management app. The developer can be contacted using the email address above. The legal identity shown on the final App Store listing should be confirmed before publication.

Vehicle records and local storage

APM is designed to store vehicle details, service and maintenance history, expenses, photographs, invoices, attachments and reminders locally on your device. The inspected app does not operate an APM-hosted server for storing your garage records.

Camera, documents and invoice text recognition

APM accesses photographs, documents or the camera when you choose to use those features and grant the necessary permissions. Invoice text recognition uses Google ML Kit. The invoice image and recognised text are processed on-device by ML Kit, according to Google's documentation; the SDK may nevertheless transmit technical metrics, including device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, event types and error codes, for diagnostics and usage analytics. See Google's ML Kit iOS data disclosures and ML Kit Terms & Privacy.

Location and fuel-price lookups

If you request nearby fuel prices, APM may request your foreground location permission. The inspected fuel-price provider integration sends latitude, longitude, fuel type and search radius to Cheapest Fuel Near Me in order to return nearby prices. You may instead enter a UK postcode; in that case APM sends the postcode to postcodes.io to resolve it to a location. External service providers may also receive standard network information such as an IP address. APM's inspected implementation does not perform continuous background location tracking.

EV charging information and directions

The inspected app downloads public EV charging location and tariff information from char.gy. The inspected requests do not explicitly pass your device's coordinates. If you request directions, destination coordinates are passed to Apple Maps. These external providers operate their own services and privacy policies.

Subscriptions and purchases

In-app subscriptions and payments are handled by Apple. APM checks purchase entitlements to control access to paid features. Apple's privacy terms apply to its purchase and payment processing.

Notifications

If you enable optional vehicle reminders, APM uses device notification functionality. You can change notification permissions through your device settings.

Encrypted backups

APM lets you create password-encrypted backups that you choose where to save or share. APM does not upload those backups to an APM-operated backup server. Any third-party storage or sharing service you select may handle the backup under its own privacy terms. The developer cannot recover a forgotten backup password.

Data deletion and retention

Vehicle records can be deleted in the app. Uninstalling APM normally deletes locally stored app data, subject to device backup and operating-system behaviour. Exported backup files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them separately. Third-party service providers may retain their own technical or request data under their policies.

Advertising

The inspected version of APM does not display third-party advertising.

Your choices and questions

You can decline optional device permissions, use manual location entry where available and remove locally stored records. For privacy questions, contact mygarage.helpdesk.uk@gmail.com. Applicable data-protection rights depend on relevant laws and the processing concerned.

Updates

We may update this policy if APM's features or data handling change. The posted policy will show its latest revision date.
